The minimum viable product is the least amount of code and assets that are 
required to play-test the game. It is designed to give the developers a 
chance to assess the quality and direction of the game.

<!--more-->

## Minimum Viable Product (MVP)

The first step of development, after requirements gathering, will be to
produce a minimum viable product (MVP).

Included in the MVP will be the following features:

1. The game will automatically play itself with no user interaction 
   [details](../features/ft-001-autoplay)
2. Universe generation. [details](../features/ft-002-universe-generation)
